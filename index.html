FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

If your question is not here, it is still worth a quick call.

How often should an older Woodstock home have ducts cleaned?

Two to three years suits the brick-home stock here, and shorter after a renovation, a pet change, or evidence of pests. Dust returning within days of a wipe means the returns are past due.

Are your technicians certified and insured?

Yes, certified and fully insured on every job. Ontario does not issue a trade licence for duct cleaning, so certification, training, and insurance are the meaningful credentials, and ours are in force.

How do you prove the cleaning happened?

Photos of your duct interiors from before the work and after it, reviewed with you on site before we pack up. We would rather show you than bill you on faith.

Dryer struggling through loads. Machine or vent?

The vent, most times. Woodstock basement and wall runs collect lint until airflow chokes while the machine keeps heating. We clear the full path and measure flow at the hood, and we will say plainly if the appliance itself looks responsible.

What does the full cleaning involve and how long?

The system goes under negative pressure, registers come out and get washed, every branch is brushed section by section, the blower compartment is cleaned, and photos close the visit. Typical Woodstock homes run three to five hours.

That first-furnace-start smell, can it be removed?

Yes. It rides on residue inside the system, which cleaning removes, and a sanitizer pass over the clean surfaces handles the rest. Done in that order it does not come back with the season.

Why does the house dust over regardless of cleaning?

Usually return-side leakage, with the furnace drawing air from the cellar or cavities instead of the rooms. We inspect and seal for that specifically, before another cleaning is worth anything.

Do you serve the county beyond Woodstock?

Yes. Ingersoll, Tillsonburg, Embro, Tavistock, Norwich, and Stratford are all regular territory, plus the surrounding townships. Confirm rural addresses when booking.
