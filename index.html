Questions About Your Woodstock Request

Why should I include make-up air and rooftop equipment in my Woodstock request?

If the building has make-up air units or rooftop equipment, identify them separately and ask how they will be assessed. Access, equipment configuration, and operating requirements can change the scope; confirm which components are included before comparing proposals.

For dryer vent cleaning in Woodstock, how should I map bends and wall sections?

Long routes and concealed bends can affect the access method. If the vent passes through a basement ceiling or older wall, share any available layout information and ask how the proposed equipment follows the route without damaging the duct.

How can I compare the supply and return scope for a Woodstock property?

Ask which supply branches, return runs, trunk sections, and accessible components are included. If a home has been renovated or duct routes are unclear, a simple layout sketch and photos of visible openings can help clarify the proposed scope and identify areas that may need separate access.

How can I confirm HRV core and drain scope before work in Woodstock?

If an HRV is installed, ask whether the core and screens are included and how the condensate path will be inspected. A drainage concern may need attention beyond cleaning, so note visible water, staining, or a blocked drain and share those observations.

How can I confirm leaks before sealing before work in Woodstock?

Ask how suspected openings will be inspected and which accessible joints are included in the repair estimate. If earlier renovations changed the duct layout, provide photos or plans. Confirming the location first helps avoid treating sound sections while missing the actual source.
